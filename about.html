About Our Operations

The iScholars International Academy ICT Club provides students with practical technology workflows. We emphasize critical logic processing alongside regular technical exercises.

OUR MISSION: To provide qualitative, practical based education guided by the quran and the teachings of the prophet Muhammad (SAW)

Club Core Targets

We aim to remove the intimidation factors behind software engineering. Our members learn to analyze live web programs, document processes, and deploy code packages cleanly.

VISION: To develop muslim children into erudite scholars who are grounded in all spheres of knowledge, and are fully capable of leading the worldin the light of the Quran and sunnah

Weekly Activities

  • Interface Prototyping: Designing wireframes for desktop applications.
  • Logic Build Scrimmages: Fast-paced structural coding sprints.
  • Hardware Setup Runs: Assembling network kits and logic micro-units.
